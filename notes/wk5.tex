\section{More Regression Stuff}

In this section, we consider mainly the following dataset:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Lalonde Data from \textcite{dehejia1999causal}
    \item Evaluation of the labor market program in 1968(i.e., treatment date is 1968)
    \item It has two datasets:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Experimental}: randomized treatment assignment to the labor market program(185 T \& 260 C)
        \item \textbf{Observational(CPS)}: control group from the CPS survey (185 T \& 15,992 C)
    \end{itemize}
    \item Outcome Variable: Earnings in 1978(two yrs after the treatment)
    \item Other Variables:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Program Participation
        \item Age
        \item Earnings in 1974/1975
        \item Children
        \item Education
        \item Ethnicity
        \item etc.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

The main question is \textit{whether the labor market program is effective in increasing the earnings of the participants}.
